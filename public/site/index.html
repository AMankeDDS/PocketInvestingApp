Invest together

Start or join a group with roles, proposals and votes. The Lead Trader’s trades reach members as approval requests, or a club can pool its money and trade by vote.

  1. A trade is placed

    By an investor you copy, or your group’s Lead Trader.

  2. You get a request

    Sized to your own account, with a notification on your phone.

  3. You decide

    Approve it, take half, or skip it. The choice is always yours.

  4. It fills in your account

    Every trade is recorded and shows up on your statements.

Run your investment club here.

Clubs have run on spreadsheets, email threads and a shared brokerage login for decades. Pocket keeps the money, the decisions and the records together.

Pooled club accounts

Members add money and own units of the club. Every trade is decided by a member vote.

Club books

Unit values, dues and withdrawals, with a Treasurer role and a monthly close that a second officer signs off.

Meetings and votes

A live agenda with open votes, what changed since last time, attendance and minutes.

Ideas and research

A shared watchlist and stock studies that turn into proposals when the group is ready.

Statements and tax worksheets

Monthly statements for every member and K-1 worksheets for your club’s tax preparer.

Money controls

Two approvals on every withdrawal, and nobody approves their own. Every change is logged.

Pooled club accounts use practice money during the beta. Clubs that keep their money at their own brokerage can use the books, meetings and statements today.

Questions

Is this real money?

Not yet. During the beta, every account uses practice money, so you can learn the app and try groups with nothing at risk. Real-money accounts come next, held at a registered broker-dealer.

Will anyone trade in my account automatically?

No. When someone you copy trades, or your group’s Lead Trader does, you get a request sized to your account. Nothing happens until you approve it.

Can my investment club use Pocket?

Yes. Your club can keep its books, run meetings and votes, and send members statements here. Pooled club accounts are available with practice money during the beta.

Does Pocket give investment advice?

No. Pocket is a place to invest, share ideas and decide together. Track records and studies are information, not recommendations, and past results don’t predict future returns.

How do I get in?

The beta is invite only. Request an invite below, or join through a link from a member of a group. You can also try the demo right away, no account needed.
