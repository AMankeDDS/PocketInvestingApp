About Pocket

Investing is more fun, and often wiser, with other people. Pocket makes that easy without giving up control.

What Pocket is

Pocket is a social investing app. You can invest on your own, follow investors you trust and copy their trades with your approval, or invest together with friends, family or an investment club that votes on what to buy.

What we believe

  • Your account, your decision. Nothing trades without your approval.
  • Groups need structure. Clear roles, voting rules and shared records keep groups fair.
  • Honesty about risk. No hype, no promises, and sample data is always labeled.

Contact

Questions, ideas or press: andrew@pocketinvesting.com. Pocket is based in Connecticut.
